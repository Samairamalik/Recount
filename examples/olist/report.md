# 2017 E-Commerce Marketplace Annual Performance Review

## Executive Summary & Full-Year Milestones

The 2017 fiscal year marked a period of rapid expansion for our Brazilian e-commerce marketplace, characterized by steady quarter-over-quarter volume gains and expanding geographic coverage. Across all 27 states, the platform processed 43,428 orders and generated 6,921,535.24 in total revenue. Commercial performance remained stable throughout the year, maintaining an average order value of 159.38. Operational logistics delivered strong results across diverse geographic regions, keeping the national average delivery time at 12.98 days—a turnaround speed that remained well under two weeks despite significant order volume spikes in the second half of the year.

## Quarterly Growth & Seasonal Performance

The business opened the year with a solid foundation in the first quarter, recording 4,949 orders and 813,052.64 in revenue, while maintaining an average delivery turnaround of 13.09 days. Sao Paulo took the lead in the first quarter, establishing early commercial momentum that laid the groundwork for rapid operational scaling throughout the remainder of the year.

Commercial acceleration occurred rapidly in the second quarter, as order volume nearly doubled. Specifically, order volume surged by 81.53% quarter-over-quarter to reach 8,984 orders, which in turn drove revenue growth of 78.06% to hit 1,447,714.17. Despite this rapid operational expansion, delivery performance improved, bringing the average delivery time down to 12.55 days.

The third quarter maintained strong upward momentum, generating 1,913,208.93 in gross revenue across 12,215 orders. This expansion reflected solid quarter-over-quarter growth of 32.15% in revenue and 35.96% in order volume. The third quarter also achieved the peak fulfillment efficiency of the year, reducing average delivery times to a year-low of 11.41 days.

The year concluded with a record-breaking fourth quarter, propelled by seasonal holiday shopping. Fourth-quarter revenue increased by 43.61% over the prior quarter to reach 2,747,559.5, while order count expanded by 41.47% to peak at 17,280 orders. However, the volume surge created temporary fulfillment friction, causing average delivery times to stretch to 14.28 days.

## Regional Distribution and State Rankings

Sales distribution across Brazil remained heavily concentrated in the Southeast and South regions, with six key states dominating overall platform activity. Sao Paulo served as the primary economic engine for the marketplace, generating 4,228,002.62 in revenue across 17,071 orders—representing roughly two-fifths of all orders at 39.31%. Sao Paulo also set the national benchmark for fulfillment speed, achieving a remarkably fast average delivery time of just 9.3 days.

Rio de Janeiro secured the second position overall, generating 1,012,943.05 in revenue from 5,968 orders, which accounted for 13.74% of total orders nationwide while averaging 14.74 days for delivery. Minas Gerais followed closely in third place, capturing a 12.07% order share with 5,240 orders that generated 825,060.95 in revenue, supported by an average delivery time of 12.08 days.

Southern states also contributed meaningfully to national volume and top-line performance. Rio Grande do Sul generated 411,077.38 in revenue across 2,591 orders, capturing 5.97% of total orders with an average delivery time of 15.23 days. Parana logged 2,192 orders for a 5.05% order share and 336,300.71 in total revenue, averaging 12.4 days for delivery fulfillment. Finally, Santa Catarina captured a 3.81% share of total order volume with 1,653 orders, generating 260,144.81 in gross revenue while recording an average delivery time of 15.33 days.
