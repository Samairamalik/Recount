# Executive Overview of 2025 Chicago Taxi Operations

During the full calendar year of 2025, Chicago taxi activity reached a total trip volume of 6,825,838 trips and generated total revenue of $184,317,971.69. Out of this full-year financial output in 2025, overall fare revenue accounted for $151,149,474.60, while total driver tips reached $18,053,912.92. Across all rides completed in 2025, drivers maintained an average trip distance of 6.44 miles, an average fare of $22.18, and an overall average trip value of $27.05.

# Quarterly Trajectory and Monthly Trends

Demand experienced significant movement across the four quarters of the year. In the first quarter of 2025, total trip volume stood at 1,437,471 trips, yielding total revenue of $38,321,110.85. Activity expanded rapidly entering spring, as total revenue grew by 36.06% in the second quarter of 2025 compared to the first quarter of 2025, reaching a quarterly total revenue of $52,139,964.79 on 1,875,989 total trips. Following this peak, total trip volume contracted by 4.12% in the third quarter of 2025 compared to the second quarter of 2025, recording 1,798,636 total trips and $48,950,641.03 in quarterly total revenue. By the fourth quarter of 2025, overall quarterly trip volume decreased further to 1,713,742 trips. Average trip duration also varied by period, peaking at an average trip duration of 1,263.31 seconds in the second quarter of 2025 compared to a low average trip duration of 1,146.51 seconds in the fourth quarter of 2025.

At a monthly level, January 2025 registered the year's lowest monthly total trip count of 424,927 trips. Ride volume climbed consistently through spring until May 2025, which served as the peak month for ride activity with a monthly total trip count of 651,900 trips and monthly total revenue of $18,230,063.02.

# Payment Preferences and Tipping Behavior

An analysis of passenger settlement methods shows clear preferences across payment types. Credit Card ranked as the primary payment method in 2025, representing a 35.42% share of total trips with 2,417,767 trips and generating $93,655,145.68 in total revenue. Mobile payments ranked second by volume in 2025, capturing a 25.85% share of total trips with 1,764,168 trips, while Cash ranked third with a 24.12% share of total trips representing 1,646,332 trips.

Tipping behaviors varied dramatically depending on how passengers paid. Credit Card passengers recorded an average tip of $6.07 per trip in 2025, whereas Mobile payment transactions yielded an average tip of $1.88 per trip in 2025. Credit Card tipping levels fluctuated moderately across quarters, rising from an average tip of $5.97 per trip in the first quarter of 2025 to a peak average tip of $6.20 per trip in the second quarter of 2025, before settling at an average tip of $5.99 per trip in the fourth quarter of 2025.

# Vendor Performance and Market Structure

Market share among Chicago taxi operators remained concentrated among a few major vendors. Flash Cab achieved the highest market share in 2025, capturing a 20.49% share of total trips with 1,398,471 trips and producing $35,269,287.71 in total revenue. Taxicab Insurance Agency Llc ranked second in trip volume with a 12.28% share of total trips representing 837,932 trips in 2025. On a quarterly basis, Flash Cab hit its operational peak in the second quarter of 2025, delivering 380,759 trips and earning total revenue of $9,721,755.14.

Pricing and trip profiles varied across top operators throughout the year. Among top providers, 5 Star Taxi recorded the highest average trip value in 2025 at $31.22 per trip along with an average trip distance of 7.86 miles. Conversely, Tac - Yellow Cab Association recorded the lowest average trip distance among top companies in 2025 at 1.96 miles per trip, resulting in an average trip value of $20.78 per trip in 2025.
