# Learning log
One line per session: something I learned, or a question an interviewer would ask about what I built today.
Sunday ritual: (1) did this week's milestone-state come to exist? (2) what could I be interviewed on? (3) any stop-and-reconsider trigger active?

- 2026-09-06 — Stage 0, design consequence 1/3: **direction/sign must be a checked field** on growth and comparison claims. Motivating case from the spike: "Fourth-quarter revenue declined by 43.61%" verified as PASS, because the comparator checked magnitude only and never read "declined" — the true change was +43.61%. A false accept on a claim asserting the opposite of the truth. Interviewer question: how does your verifier tell a wrong magnitude apart from a correct magnitude with an inverted sign?
- 2026-09-06 — Stage 0, design consequence 2/3: **`subject` is first-class on every claim type**, not just `ranking`. 13 of 39 extracted claims had spans naming no entity at all ("336,300.71 in total revenue"); the structured `subject` field was the sole carrier. Span-local verification would have computed the national figure and produced 13 false flags.
- 2026-09-06 — Stage 0, design consequence 3/3: **extractor recall is weakest on non-numeric claims**. Of 13 genuine extraction misses, 9 were the direction-only comparisons, the rankings, and the vague growth — every claim with no number in the sentence. Since several of those are deterministically checkable, that is lost coverage, not correct abstention. Per spike/RESULTS.md.
- 2026-09-06 — Stage 1, BINDING Stage 3 requirement (a): **better/worse comparisons require metric polarity** (`polarity` in SemanticConfig) and must abstain `schema_gap` when it is absent. Fixture c17 "delivery performance improved" is only checkable because the config says lower `avg_delivery_days` is better; a silent higher-is-better default would be a false-accept path. Not implemented in Stage 1.
- 2026-09-06 — Stage 1, BINDING Stage 3 requirement (b): **ranking `group_by` may name a time grain** ("quarter", "month", "year"), resolved from `time_column`, not from `entities`. Fixture c23 "peak fulfillment efficiency of the year" ranks quarters by `avg_delivery_days` and must NOT abstain as `schema_gap`. Not implemented in Stage 1.
- 2026-09-06 — Stage 1: a Pydantic discriminated union dispatches on one literal field (`type`) before validating anything else, so an unknown type fails fast with a one-line error instead of five overlapping ones, and mypy narrows `Claim` to the exact class after an `isinstance`. Interviewer question: why `extra="forbid"` on a contract that sits between an LLM and a verifier?
- 2026-09-06 — Stage 2, AMENDMENT (visible, not silent): `AbstainReason` gained a fourth value, `NO_DATA`, for an empty slice, a zero growth baseline, or a ranking subject absent from the groups. The Stage 1 freeze was on the corruption taxonomy, not this enum; the enum's design rule is "grouped by what the user can do about it", and NO_DATA's action ("your data does not cover this period/entity") is distinct from unsupported type, schema gap and vagueness. It is the only reason the engine (not the compiler) raises.
- 2026-09-06 — Stage 2: a SQL prepared statement can bind values but never identifiers, so "one parameterized template per plan type" is honest only with the rule spelled out: column names are rendered from the plan after the loader validated them against the real schema and execute() re-checks them; keywords (SUM/AVG/COUNT, ASC/DESC, the optional entity clause) come from Literal fields; everything data-shaped is a bound parameter. Also: the half-ulp comparison is done in Decimal because 12.555 - 12.55 in floats is not 0.005. Interviewer question: why can't `$column` be a parameter, and what would go wrong if you quoted it yourself without the schema check?
